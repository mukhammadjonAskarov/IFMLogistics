A carrier built on answering the phone.

IFM Logistics Inc is a truckload carrier based in Rolling Meadows, Illinois, operated by SIP Xpress Inc.

Who we are

Freight moved by people who pick up.

IFM Logistics Inc moves dry van, reefer, and power-only truckload freight for shippers and brokers across the country. We're a mid-size fleet — big enough to cover real capacity, small enough that your load doesn't disappear into a call queue.

We also run one of the larger company-driver programs in our size class, because a carrier is only as reliable as the drivers behind the wheel.

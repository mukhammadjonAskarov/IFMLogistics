Who we are

IFM Logistics Inc ("IFM Logistics," "we," "us") is a truckload carrier operated by SIP Xpress Inc, based at 1600 Golf Rd, Ste 1256, Rolling Meadows, IL 60008. This policy covers information we collect through ifmlogistics.com, by phone, and by text message when we communicate with shippers, brokers, drivers, and job applicants.

What we collect

What you give us. When you fill out a quote or driver application form, or contact us, we collect your name, company, email address, phone number, and whatever freight or driving details you include.

Text message information. If you opt in to text messaging, we collect your mobile number, the date and time of opt-in, and a record of messages exchanged.

Automatic information. Our site may use basic analytics to understand traffic, such as pages visited and general location by IP address.

How we use it

  • Respond to quote requests and driver applications
  • Coordinate pickups, deliveries, and shipment updates
  • Communicate with drivers about job opportunities and onboarding
  • Improve our website and services

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers who help us operate (such as our website host or SMS platform) only as needed to provide our services, and when required by law.

Mobile information, SMS opt-in data, and text-message consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging

Texting is optional. You can request a quote, apply to drive, or work with us without agreeing to receive text messages.

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our contact or driver application form, or by texting us first. See our full SMS Terms for details on message frequency, costs, and how to opt out.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal, accounting, and safety record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text message to opt out at any time. Call +1 (765) 222-4545 to ask what information we have about you or to request it be deleted, subject to our legal recordkeeping obligations.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our services are for businesses and individuals 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent version.
